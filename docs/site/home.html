Turn your portable DAC into a wireless audio receiver.

Wavelink sends a computer's live audio over your local Wi-Fi to a phone, tablet, or other device — where it plays through ordinary output or a connected USB DAC. Free lossy mode for everyone; lossless Pro mode that is honestly measured, never assumed.

What you get

Emitter apps capture audio; receiver apps render it — lossy or lossless depending on your tier.

🎧

Wi-Fi relay

Direct, local, peer-to-peer. Lossy Opus (Free) or lossless FLAC/raw PCM with sample-perfect integrity proven by hash tests (Pro).

🔌

USB DAC support

Receivers detect and route to a portable USB DAC where the OS exposes it (Android TYPE_USB_DEVICE, iOS usbAudio, macOS CoreAudio).

🔒

Private by default

No cloud, no accounts, no telemetry. Authenticated, encrypted local streaming; redacted diagnostics that never leak audio, secrets, or raw device identities.

How it works

Connect

Plug a portable DAC into any receiver device.

Pair

Launch the receiver, become discoverable, and approve an authenticated pairing request from an emitter.

Listen

Play from the emitter; the receiver renders live audio to the DAC. Fidelity status is honest — “bit-perfect” only after hardware verification.

Now and next

Honest status: the reference system (headless emitter + receiver over QUIC) is verified end-to-end — lossless hash-perfect under clean and 1% lossy networks, 60-minute clean soak PASS, 215 tests. The app shells implement each platform's capture, output routing, policy, consent, and status surfaces and are being wired to that proven transport via a FrameSink seam. Hardware-only checks (real capture, USB-DAC hotplug, Bluetooth lab, bit-perfect loopback) and store signing are pending external gates with runbooks — nothing here overclaims them.
